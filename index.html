My Vida, I don't think a single webpage could ever hold all the things I want to tell you, but I wanted to try. I love you more than I sometimes know how to explain. You mean so much to me, and I hope my words and actions keep reminding you of that.

I don't want my love to be only pretty words on a screen. Vida, thank you for being you. Thank you for the moments we've shared and for the ones still waiting for us.
